Privacy by Design

How PravasiShram AI decouples identity from movement events using cryptographic disassociation and DPDP Act 2023 principles.

1. Explicit Consent

Voluntary participation at check-in with clear disclosure of purpose and no punitive lockouts.

2. Data Minimisation

We extract only the migration event (From, To, Trade). No background telemetry or device fingerprinting.

3. Purpose Limitation

Information is strictly limited to welfare planning and cannot be repurposed for personal tracking, tax enforcement, or commercial sharing.

4. Strict Access Control

Disaggregated data is inaccessible to field officers; only aggregated statistics are published.

5. Aggregated Analytics

Heatmaps only render corridor paths when minimum threshold events (k > 50) are achieved.

6. 1-Click Consent Withdrawal

Citizens can withdraw consent at any time, initiating automated cryptographic erasure.

Data Use & Privacy Disclosure

Under the proposed PravasiShram AI framework, voluntary migration check-ins are handled under strict privacy-by-design standards:

  • No Continuous Tracking: The system logs only single episodic transit events. No GPS background processes exist.
  • Identity Disassociation: Once an event is confirmed, individual identifiers are stripped before aggregation.
  • Purpose Limitation: Aggregated corridor data is used exclusively to assist destination welfare capacity planning.
  • Retention & Purge: Episodic event entries have an automated 90-day time-to-live (TTL) lifecycle.
